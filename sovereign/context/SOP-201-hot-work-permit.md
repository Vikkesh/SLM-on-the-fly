# SOP-201: Hot Work Permit

Applies to: welding, cutting, grinding, open flame or spark-producing work anywhere inside the
refinery battery limit.

## 2. Preconditions

- Gas test within 30 minutes of start: LEL must read 0%; H2S < 5 ppm; O2 19.5-23.5%.
- Fire watch assigned for the duration and 30 minutes after completion.
- Permit valid for a single shift (max 8 hours); re-issue requires a fresh gas test.
- Drains within 15 m sealed or covered with wet sacking.

## 3. Roles

Permit Issuer: Shift Superintendent. Permit Receiver: Maintenance Supervisor. Authorising
Officer for Zone 1 areas: Fire & Safety Officer.

## 4. Suspension

Any gas alarm, change in wind direction toward the work area, or a plant upset suspends the
permit immediately. Restart only after a new gas test and re-signature by the Issuer.
