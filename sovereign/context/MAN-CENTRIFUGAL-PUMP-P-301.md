# Manual excerpt: Centrifugal Pump P-301 (Crude Charge Pump)

Make: KSB, model RPH 200-400. Rated: 480 m3/h at 210 m head, 2975 rpm, 315 kW motor.

## Operating limits

| Parameter | Normal | Alarm | Trip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bearing temperature (DE / NDE) | < 75 C | 85 C | 95 C |
| Vibration (velocity, RMS) | < 4.5 mm/s | 7.1 mm/s | 11.2 mm/s |
| Seal flush pressure | 2.0-3.5 bar g | < 1.5 bar g | - |
| Suction pressure | > 1.2 bar g | 0.8 bar g | 0.5 bar g |
| Discharge pressure | 18-22 bar g | > 24 bar g | - |

## Flow rate estimate

Flow through the pump can be estimated from the orifice differential pressure using
Q = 0.62 * A * sqrt(2 * dP / rho), where A is the orifice area in m2, dP in Pa and rho in kg/m3.
For crude at 40 C use rho = 860 kg/m3. Orifice bore is 120 mm.

## Maintenance intervals

Mechanical seal: inspect every 4000 h. Bearings: regrease every 2000 h, replace every 16000 h.
Coupling alignment check: after any seal or bearing work and every 8000 h.
